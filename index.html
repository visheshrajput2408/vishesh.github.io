§ 01

About

I am a PhD scholar in Data Science and Engineering at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Bhopal, currently visiting Dr. Guha Balakrishnan's group at Rice University as part of the Mehta Rice Engineering Scholars Program.

My research focuses on building robust and trustworthy vision systems — from defending models against adversarial patch attacks to designing interpretable methods that explain when and why a model fails. My work has appeared at CVPR, AISTATS, ICML, IJCB, AAAI, and in IEEE Transactions on Big Data.

Before the PhD, I trained as a mathematician (M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics, IIEST Shibpur) and worked briefly as a Junior Research Fellow at DA-IICT Gandhinagar on dynamical sampling theory.
